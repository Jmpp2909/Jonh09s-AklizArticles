- **Macaw's Holidays**

**Version:** Supports 1.16+, actively updated (250+ decorations as of 1.1.0)

**THE BIG DRAW — Massive Vanilla-Friendly Decoration Set:**

Pure cosmetics with zero gameplay changes. 250+ Christmas and Halloween decorations, all craftable with simple vanilla recipes. No performance hit—perfect for multiplayer servers and pure vanilla survival worlds that want festive flair without complexity.

**DECORATIVE PIECES (The Stars):**

*Christmas Trees*
Plain and ornament-laden variants. Each comes as 4 pieces you can mix-and-match to customize height and fullness. Build signature holiday centerpieces without touching performance.

*Presents & Stockings*
Tiny, medium, and large gift boxes in multiple colors. Single or double stockings (5 colors). Pure aesthetic charm—perfect for tree bases and mantelpieces.

*Snowmen & Holiday Figures*
Pre-built snowmen (some wearing funny accessories like frying pans, some angry expressions). Super cute and instantly recognizable. Stock your landscape with character.

*Lights & Garlands*
Multiple garland styles (wavy and straight), white or green color options. String lights in all colors. Wall-mounted light variants. Mix them to turn trees into luminous centerpieces or drape bases in festive flair.

*Ornaments & Decorative Accents*
Colorful hanging ornaments, festive balloons, bells. Candy cane blocks (for building full-size candy canes) and pre-made candy canes. Wall stickers and deco (like window frames). Icicles for rooflines.

*Furniture & Props*
Doormats, wood piles, sleds, shovels, mistletoe (ceiling-hang). Colorful blocks perfect for holiday palettes.

**WHY MACAW'S WORKS:**

- **Plug-and-play**: No learning curve. Grab materials, craft, decorate. 
- **Massive variety**: 250+ options mean no two holiday builds need to look identical.
- **Mix with everything**: Pairs seamlessly with other mods (unlike some holiday packs).


- **Seasons Greetings**

Version: 1.21.1 (Fabric & Neo Forge)

**THE BIG DRAWS — Gameplay & Creativity:**

*Gingerbread Men (New Tameable Mob)*
The real star. Wild gingerbread men run around frantically, but catch one and it becomes a loyal companion. 4-20 heart health pool, follows like a wolf, can sit/stand. Equip them with pickaxes and they mine obsidian for you; give them swords and they form a full combat squad (legit can take down Ender Dragon, Wither, Warden). Die? Revive with 8 gingerbread crumbs. This is a game-changer for progression and exploration.

*Snow Golem Customization System*
Completely reworked. 70,000+ unique combinations with custom head blocks, secondary items (potato/beetroot/carrot for noses, wheat for hair), colored carpet scarves. Compatible with modded blocks and 3D item blocks packs. Stop snow trails with flint and steel, toggle them back with snowballs. Creative freedom on a crazy scale.

*Wreaths with Modular Building*
100+ combinations: base (8 leaves), center (bells), perimeter (berries, lights), and bows (colored carpets). Fully shearable to swap designs on the fly. Pair this with customizable lights (7 colors + multicolor) and you can decorate entire bases.

*Gift Boxes (Secret-Keeping)*
Craftable chests that hide contents in inventory (unlike Shulker boxes). Hand off surprises to friends without spoiling the gift. Dyeable bows for customization.

**BUILDING & AESTHETICS:**

*Gingerbread Architecture*
Gingerbread blocks + bricks + shingles, stairs, slabs, doors (with icing details). Pair with 4-layer icing system (side curls, top snow-like, bottom dripping). Peppermint blocks (vertical/horizontal candy canes with stairs/slabs). Packed snow variants + snow bricks + chiseled snow (snow golem carving). Icicles (breakable by arrows). Ronet painting (Northern Lights). Christmas hat (dyeable, moves with player).

*Immersive Snow*
Snow layers now pile over block edges naturally (configurable). Witch's House structure (Hansel & Gretel reference; gingerbread-styled, found in Grove/Snowy Taiga/Dark Forest).

**FOODS & LIVING SPACES:**

*Holiday Feast & Drinks*
Chocolate (2 recipes), fruit cake (10 hunger, customizable with any combo of apples/golden apples/chorus fruit/berries). Gingerbread cookies cooked into mobs. Cauldron drinks: warm milk (4 hunger, 30s Cozy effect), hot cocoa (3 min Cozy), eggnog (Strength + Nausea, configurable). Bucket versions last longer; hot cocoa clears negatives. Cozy effect grants immunity to freezing.

**UTILITY & REDSTONE:**

*Gumdrop Buttons & Blocks*
Longest redstone signal (3 seconds vs. 1.5s wood, 1s stone). 6 colors. Blocks act like soul sand (slowing) + beds (bouncy, reduce fall damage). 

- **Snowy Spirit**

**Version:** Multi-version support (Winter-themed mod)

**THE BIG DRAW — Immersion & Gameplay Fusion:**

Winter atmosphere meets functional toys. Decorative blocks, custom villager behaviors, gingerbread-themed mobs, sleds (actual sledding mechanics), and season timing all work together to make winter feel alive rather than just festive.

**GAMEPLAY MECHANICS (The MVPs):**

*Sleds — Real Sledding Physics*
New way to traverse snowy terrain. Sleds curve around obstacles smoothly (unlike boats), accelerate downhill, and can decelerate by rotating 90 degrees like real sledding. Can attach chests/Shulker boxes for inventory. Tamed wolves can pull sleds with leads for speed boost and minimal deceleration. Carpets placed on sleds prevent freezing in powdered snow. Igloos spawn with pre-loaded sled + chest containing winter loot. Transforms snowy landscapes into ski courses.

*Gingerbread Golems — Tiny Companions*
Craft by placing pumpkin on gingerbread block + gumdrop button. Follow owner like pets (won't teleport). Command to sit with shift-click. Right-click with empty stomach to take bites (they're literal cookies). Fix damaged limbs with gingerbread cookies. Water and milk hurt them (funny mechanic). Come in 16 personality colors. Naturally spawn in Gingerbread Houses (snowy structures with witches, similar to witch huts).

*Villager AI — Winter Events*
During "Winter Time" (Dec 20 - Jan 30, configurable; works with Serene Seasons if installed), villagers place wreaths on doors and presents near meeting points. Baby villagers from Taiga/Tundra throw snowballs at entities during playtime. Adds dynamic NPC behavior without mods feeling static.

**DECORATIVE & ATMOSPHERIC:**

*Gingerbread Architecture*
Ginger crop (found wild in Sparse Jungles, craftable into Gingerbread Blocks). Candy Cane blocks (craftable into decorative variants). Wreaths (door-placeable, work with modded doors). Gumdrops (all colors, pressure-plate-like buttons on all sides).

*Ambiance Blocks*
Glow Lights (place on leaves for glowing effect, removable with shears). Snow Globe (visual that changes to snowy appearance when in snowy biomes). New winter music disc (rarely found in presents and igloo chests).

**CONFIGURABLE & MOD-FRIENDLY:**

- Highly configurable—disable/tweak any block or feature.
- Compatible with Supplementaries mod (enhanced chest/sack placement).
- Works alongside other decorative mods without conflict.


- **Winterly**

**Version:** Multi-version (Fabric loader)

**THE BIG DRAW — Lightweight Winter Cosmetics & Décor:**

Simple, charming mod focused on wearables and easy decorations. No performance hit, quick crafting, pure festive vibes without complexity. Perfect for vanilla-adjacent servers that want seasonal flair.

**WEARABLES & COSMETICS:**

*Santa Hats*
Crafted with 4 white wool + 3 blue or red wool. Zero armor value, purely cosmetic. Makes you look like a little Santa helper. Comfortable and cozy-looking.

*Scarves*
Crafted with 5 white, red, green, or blue wool. Includes rainbow variant for variety. Simple neck warmth aesthetic.

**DECORATIVE BLOCKS & PROPS:**

*Icicles & Ice Architecture*
Icicles spawn on buildings after rain/cold weather and in caves. Can craft into icicle blocks, packed icicles, icicle bars, and icicle panes. Perfect for building ice castles or frosty architectural details. Slippery like ice, adds authentic frozen aesthetic.

*Dense Snow & Snow Bricks*
Craft dense snow from snow blocks. Convert to snow bricks, with stairs and slab variants available. Great for building snowy structures with more texture variety than vanilla.

*Cryo Marble Tools*
Cryo Marble found in caves. Combine with iron sword to create cryo marble tools (same durability/power as iron, purely aesthetic). Ice King-inspired look for themed builds.

*Snowball Walls*
Crafted with 6 snowballs. Decorative walls—mobs can pass over them, so not ideal for pens, but great for snowball arenas and festive barriers. Fun and lighthearted.

*Snow Guys (Decorative Snowmen)*
Crafted with 3 snow + 2 sticks + 1 carrot. Wears little top hat. Stationary decoration (not Snow Golems). Funny and charming character pieces for landscapes.

**FESTIVE DÉCOR:**

*Garlands*
Two types: standard garlands (3 glowstone pieces + 3 red/green/blue dye) and rainy garlands (6 glowstone + string). Standard garlands change colors dynamically; rainy garlands sparkle and don't change colors. Works anywhere—trees, houses, builds. Visually distinctive.

*Gift Boxes/Presents*
Crafted with paper (qty varies by color) + dye. Right-click to store up to 3 items inside. Breaks to release contents—perfect for Secret Santa or friend surprises. Available in white, red, green, yellow.

**WHY WINTERLY WORKS:**

- **Quick crafting**: Simple recipes using vanilla materials.
- **Mix-and-match aesthetic**: Pairs easily with Macaw's Holidays and other mods.

- **Maiden's Merry Making** (AKLIZ PARTNER MOD)

**Version:** Multi-version (Requires GeckoLib; Fabric & Forge; 1.21.1 also requires Patchouli & Mama's Herbs and Harvest)

**THE BIG DRAW — Festival-Ready Immersive Holiday Decorations:**

Massive mod packed with interactive decorations, wearables, and foods across multiple winter holidays. Perfect for community events, immersive servers, and players who want full holiday atmosphere. Builds feel lived-in with fireplaces, customizable trees, wreaths, and cultural celebration blocks.

**INTERACTIVE CHRISTMAS TREES:**

*Small Spruce Trees (Natural Generation)*
Found in cold biomes. Cut down to get Christmas Tree item. Dye with white dye for snowy variants. Requires craft stand to display.

*3-Section Customizable Trees*
Each tree section accepts: Lights (white or multicolored), Ornaments (white or bright), Sweet Berry Garland. Tree Toppers: Star or Bow variants. Click sections to add decorations; click items to remove. Full personalization per tree.

**FESTIVE LIGHTS & GARLANDS:**

*Light Variants (All Colors)*
Classic, Mini, Icicle, Twinkling Icicle. White or multicolor sets. Horizontal, diagonal, and roof-cap blocks. Lights connect around corners. Perfect for rooflines and building perimeters.

*Garlands & Wreaths*
Plain, white, or multicolored. Garlands attach to walls and picket fences. Lit Wreaths attach to doors (giving off light). Can be crafted into decorated doors/fences ahead of time.

*Lighted Leaves*
Vanilla leaves + lights = glowing foliage. Instant tree decoration without building.

**FIREPLACE & MANTEL SYSTEM:**

*Fireplace Mantels*
3x3x1 pre-built blocks. Decorable with horizontal garlands (any type) and stockings. Click with garland to add; click empty to remove. Creates cozy winter focal points.

*Mantel-Top Decorations*
Tall candle holders, lanterns with flowers, small trees with ornaments, gingerbread houses, "Joy" frame sets. Sized perfectly for mantel display but work anywhere.

**ARCHITECTURAL:**

*Picket Fences*
Craftable from any vanilla wood. Garland-compatible. Wreath-compatible gates. Build festive fence lines throughout villages.

*Lamp Posts*
Stack vertically. 2+ blocks high allows wreath placement. Light with torch on top. Create street-level holiday illumination.

*Snow-Covered Roofs*
Combine roof tiles with stairs/slabs from any vanilla wood. Auto-snow or manual snowball application. Realistic tiled roofing.

**WEARABLES & COSMETICS:**

*Ugly Sweater Armor*
4 tiers: Leather (crafted), Iron/Diamond/Netherite (smithing upgrades). 2 color options (mix-and-match for cringe factor). Upgrade templates found in various chests.

*Mobs in Ugly Sweaters*
Zombies, Skeletons, Creepers, Zombie Piglins naturally spawn wearing sweaters and Santa hats. Festive hostile encounters.

**FOODS & CROPS:**

*Holiday Crops*
Corn, Sweet Potato, Ginger, Mint (biome-specific; found in village chests). Grow for holiday recipes.

*Festive Dishes*
Stuffed Poultry Dinner, Holiday Ham Dinner, Christmas Cookies, Gingerbread Cookie, Cookie Tray, Fruit Cake, Sweet Potato Pie, Fudge, Hot Cocoa (with peppermint variant), Eggnog. Many can be crafted into placeable, edible blocks.

*Holiday Music Discs*
2 discs: Lofi Holiday + Traditional Holiday songs. Found in Igloo and Dungeon chests.

**CULTURAL HOLIDAY BLOCKS:**

*Hanukkah*
Menorah (blue/white candles in order; light with flint & steel), Dreidel (decorative), Gelt (decorative), Rugelach (food).

*Kwanzaa*
Mkeka (tiledbase), Kinara (holds red/green/black candles), Chalice, Harvest Tray. Corn/Sweet Potato plantable on Mkeka. Mkeka holds up to 3 items.

**PRESENTS & MISC:**

*Randomized Presents*
Found in dungeon/mineshaft chests. Break to reveal random loot (good to exceptional gifts). Customizable via datapack for 1.21.4+.

**WHY MAIDEN'S WORKS:**

- **Comprehensive**: Every holiday need in one mod (trees, lights, food, armor, multiple cultures).
- **Roleplay-heavy**: Mantel system, interactive trees, and cultural blocks encourage storytelling.
- **Event-ready**: Perfect for server-wide holiday celebrations and competitions.
- **Akliz partner**: Built with multiplayer servers in mind.
